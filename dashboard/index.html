Welcome to Driver Routes

Plan each driver's day in three steps. Everything updates instantly.
  1. Pick a day in the week strip at the top. Each day keeps its own plan, so you can map the week ahead.
  2. Your day. Say how many drivers you have. The defaults for time per stop, speed and day length work for most days.
  3. Where to go. The black D on the map is the depot, where every driver starts: drag it to move it. Then choose Whole parishes or Specific terminals (search, or press Browse all).
  4. Routes. Each driver gets a colour, a numbered list and a one-tap Navigate button. Press Edit stop (or click a pin on the map) to change a stop's number, give it to another driver, see the closest locations, swap or remove it. On a computer you can also drag stops up and down a route, or onto another driver: each driver shows how many km the move saves or adds.

What you'll see

D
Depot — the starting point. Draggable.
S
Start stop — appears when you start from a selected terminal instead of the depot.
3
Numbered pin — the 3rd stop on that driver's route. Each driver has their own colour.
OVER DAY
This route is longer than the working day. Add a driver or remove stops.
APPROX
The map pin is only approximate. Navigation links search by address, so they are still accurate.
REMOTE
A group is too far from the depot for a day trip.
